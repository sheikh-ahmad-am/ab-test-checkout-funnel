Shipping a redesign because it "feels better" is quietly costing you money.

I A/B tested a new checkout flow on 20,000 users, 50/50 split.

Headline result: purchases went 11.98% to 14.50%. A lift of 2.52 points, 95% CI [+1.58, +3.45], p-value near zero.

Verdict: ship it. Right?

Not so fast. I segmented by device and the story split in two. Desktop gained 7.00 points. Mobile was flat and wasn't statistically significant.

So here's my real recommendation: ship to desktop, hold mobile back for its own diagnosis. The funnel backed it up. The lift landed exactly between checkout-started and payment-done (+8.06pp), right where a checkout redesign should bite.

One more thing the data gave me: first-touch attribution rewards discovery (social), last-touch rewards closing (paid). Same funnel, two different stories about what "worked."

The lesson I keep relearning: the overall number's never the whole story. Segment before you conclude.
